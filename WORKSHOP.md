[h1]Raid Extension (rimhunter)[/h1]

Four new hostile incidents beyond raids: poachers, logging crews, hostile trader caravans and hostile travelers. Works on RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7 from one subscription.

This is a continuation of Shadowrabbit's Raid Extension: [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2554928098]Shadowrabbit's Workshop page[/url] and [url=https://github.com/Shadowrabbit/RaidExtension]Shadowrabbit's source on GitHub[/url]. It is built from Shadowrabbit's own source (the last commit, 2022-10-22). Where this version uses work from the 1.6 fork by llunak, the changelog and the commit name the author.

[h2]What it does[/h2]

This mod adds 4 new incidents. These events expand the ways in which hostile factions interact with colonists, making them not limited to the form of raids.

[list]
[*]Poaching: hostile hunters come to kill the large animals near your colony and carry the prey away.
[*]Logging: a hostile crew fells the trees on your map and leaves with the wood.
[*]Hostile caravan passing: a trader caravan of a hostile faction passes through your area with its guards.
[*]Lost raider (hostile traveler): a small hostile group travels through your area.
[/list]

The mod needs no Harmony and no other mod.
