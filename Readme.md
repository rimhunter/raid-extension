# Raid Extension (rimhunter)

A continuation of [Raid Extension](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2554928098) by Shadowrabbit, for RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7, by rimhunter. It is built from Shadowrabbit's own source, [Shadowrabbit/RaidExtension](https://github.com/Shadowrabbit/RaidExtension), as of its last commit (5f91103, 2022-10-22). llunak's 1.6 fork ([llunak/rimworld-RaidExtension](https://github.com/llunak/rimworld-RaidExtension)) and Ionfrigate12345's 1.5 continuation ([Ionfrigate12345/Rimworld_RaidExtensionContinued](https://github.com/Ionfrigate12345/Rimworld_RaidExtensionContinued)) are credited where their work is used; each such change is named in `CHANGELOG.md`. rimhunter claims no ownership of the original, and will hand the mod back or take it down at Shadowrabbit's request.

This mod adds 4 new incidents: poachers who hunt large animals near your colony, logging crews that fell trees, hostile trader caravans passing through, and hostile travelers. It needs no Harmony and no other mod.

Do not run another Raid Extension build alongside this one. RimWorld's mod list warns you when the original, the 1.5 continuation or the 1.6 fork is enabled together with it.
